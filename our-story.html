Our Story

SWISSTYTEA by Chimmy

Made with love. Inspired by Switzerland. Created with soul.

🇹🇭 🇱🇰 🇨🇭

Welcome to SWISSTYTEA by Chimmy — a special tea brand created with love, warmth, and a passion for beautiful moments.

Originally from Thailand, Chimmy now lives in Switzerland, a country that has become deeply meaningful to her. She chose Switzerland as the inspiration for SWISSTYTEA because she has given her heart and soul to this beautiful place. Guided by a secret ingredient passed down from her parents — love — Chimmy created SWISSTYTEA to share warmth, happiness, and sweet harmony with people around the world.

She loves both Sri Lankan tea and Thai tea, and brings these inspirations together with the beauty, quality, and spirit of Switzerland. Our teas are thoughtfully created with natural ingredients and a touch of Swiss, Sri Lankan, and Asian-inspired elegance.

The secret ingredient is love

Our Teas

🍯

Sweet Honey Black Tea

A smooth and comforting black tea blended with the natural sweetness of honey. Rich, warm, and satisfying, it is perfect for relaxing, enjoying a quiet moment, or sharing with someone special.

🍋

Ginger Honey Lemon Tea

A refreshing and warming blend of ginger, honey, and lemon. Created to offer a naturally comforting experience, it combines a bright citrus flavor with a gentle warmth.

🌼

Chamomile Tea

A gentle and soothing herbal tea with a delicate floral aroma. Perfect for peaceful moments, relaxation, and winding down at the end of the day.

🍃

Green Tea

A light and refreshing tea with a clean, delicate taste. Perfect for enjoying a calm moment and bringing a little natural freshness to your day.

At SWISSTYTEA, we believe tea is more than a drink. It is a moment of comfort, connection, and self-care. Every cup is made with love to help you relax, feel warm, and enjoy a little sweetness and harmony in your day. Our wish is to share this heart and soul with people around the world, one beautiful cup at a time.
